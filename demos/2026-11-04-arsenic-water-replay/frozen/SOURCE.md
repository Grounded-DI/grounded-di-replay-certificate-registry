# Source record — arsenic in drinking water
US Environmental Protection Agency, National Primary Drinking Water Regulations, https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/national-primary-drinking-water-regulations ; accessed 2026-11-04 (America/New_York). EPA's inorganic chemicals table lists arsenic MCL 0.010 mg/L as of 01/23/06. Relevant table excerpt: “Arsenic ... 0.010 as of 01/23/06.” Source identity here is a recorded excerpt and URL, not archived full source bytes or automatic live-source verification. The numerical MCL is an enforceable standard, not an MCL goal. EPA chemical contaminant rules page https://www.epa.gov/dwreginfo/chemical-contaminant-rules states the arsenic standard applies to community water systems and non-transient non-community water systems. EPA small-entity guide https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2021-08/compliance-dw_arsenic.pdf describes sampling at entry points after treatment, monitoring schedules, and confirmation and annual-average context; state-specific rules and directions may matter. 40 CFR 141.23 governs sampling and compliance and may include early violation conditions; we do not implement it. EPA's implementation guidance describes reporting arsenic results to nearest 0.001 mg/L for compliance (https://semspub.epa.gov/work/HQ/404270.pdf). The exact comparisons in this demonstration intentionally do NOT perform compliance rounding and are NOT regulatory determinations. Use a raw numerical comparison only. EPA's 2006 MCL date is listed in its current table; guidance documents are historical. No inference that a single sample decides compliance, no advice on safety or health risk.
