# Integrated Legal Evidence Chain Replay

This package connects four synthetic stages: BriefWise authority binding, DepoBot testimony tracing, PIDBot product/exposure separation, and VerdictBridge litigation sufficiency.

The package contains two fresh chain executions, stage outputs, final chain output, independent evaluation, altered-stage controls, downstream-injection controls, raw stdout/stderr, manifests, and a reproducibility archive.

All records are synthetic closed-world fixtures. No real-world legal, medical, product, exposure, causation, liability, external-verification, or deployment claim is made.
