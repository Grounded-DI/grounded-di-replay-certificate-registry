# Frozen replay contract — synthetic cold-chain inspection

Starting registry commit: 54b7be93bc0fe5b458b82921faf5d43ed0956c9e. Ulam benchmark SHA-256: 497614c00216d7fe86886afdfd93aa7c08a5d65a590c38dd8a78e5380d80fa98, verified against the unsigned detached receipt. This is a new demonstration, not a benchmark replay. Data and operating thresholds are synthetic; no real shipment, sensor, regulatory rule or clinical assertion is involved.

Business problem: assess whether a fictional refrigerated shipment's four recorded readings complied with a made-up temperature and data-completeness policy. Times are integer minutes from start; temperatures and offsets are integer tenths of a degree C. Calibration correction is added to each reading. The allowed range is 20..80 inclusive (2.0..8.0 C). Allowed gap between adjacent readings is at most 10 minutes, and the last reading must reach minute 30 or later. Any out-of-range reading or excessive gap or incomplete coverage produces HOLD; otherwise RELEASE. This is sample-based only: it cannot establish temperatures between measurements. Timestamps must be strictly increasing and start at minute 0; invalid data raises an error.

Frozen input: calibration +2, readings (0,38), (10,76), (20,79), (30,48); corrected values 40,78,81,50. Manually calculated expected outcome: third reading 81 is above 80, others are in range; all three gaps are 10 and coverage ends at 30; result HOLD with reason TEMPERATURE. In degrees C these are 4.0, 7.8, 8.1, 5.0. Separate calculation is literal arithmetic and policy evaluation recorded here and in tests; it shares Python and policy assumptions and is not unaffiliated verification.

Seven generated boundaries: 01 normalized input (artifact identity); 02 calibrated readings (fresh calculation); 03 temperature classifications (fresh calculation); 04 gap and coverage assessment (fresh calculation); 05 breach event ledger (fresh derivation); 06 release decision (fresh derivation); 07 integrity commitment listing SHA-256 of 01-06 (packaging/identity, not independent computation). Each process freshly generates all records; commitment derives from previously computed files. No claim that seven boundaries are seven independent systems. JSON is UTF-8, sorted keys, compact separators, ASCII escaping, final LF, integers only. Each corresponding file must match by direct bytes and SHA-256, zero tolerance. UTC timing and PID are outside identity boundaries. Python 3.12.14, Linux, standard library only, no external calls; output must not exist.

Registered tests: expected complete output, exact threshold acceptance, excessive gap hold, incomplete coverage hold, altered input changes decision, altered output rejected against commitment, coherent wrong decision with recomputed commitment passes integrity but fails fixed expected-value check, invalid timestamp rejected. Eight methods; no skips or errors. The alternate input yields RELEASE when third raw temperature becomes 78 (corrected 80). A changed output check exercises the local unsigned commitment, not a hosted enforcement system. Coherent wrong-result control alters the decision and rebuilds commitment around unchanged input; it checks that integrity is not correctness.
